Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Information stored by the game

Island Tycoon 3D currently saves game progress and daily mission information in your browser's local storage. This information is stored on your device by the browser and may be removed if you clear browser data.

Third-party resources

The game loads the Three.js library from jsDelivr to render its 3D scene. When your browser requests this resource, the third-party service may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser request data under its own policies.

Advertising and analytics

This version of the game does not have a connected rewarded-video advertising system. If advertising, analytics, or other third-party services are added later, this policy must be updated to explain the data they collect and how it is used.

Children and privacy

Parents and guardians should review the services used by the game before allowing children to use features involving third-party services.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the game's features or services change.
